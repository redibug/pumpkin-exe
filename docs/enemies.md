# Virus Enemy Roster — Physical Descriptions

Art direction: 16-bit pixel art, MMBN-style. Viruses read as malicious
software given bodies — glitchy, carved, unnatural. Pumpkin motifs run
through the roster (jam theme: PUMPKIN).

## Stalker
A gaunt, long-limbed hunter virus, matte black carapace with a carved
jack-o'-lantern grin glowing amber across its chest. Its head is a single
cyclopean eye on a ball joint that swivels independently to track the
player's row. Moves with a twitchy, stop-motion skitter on needle-thin
legs; freezes stock-still for a beat before firing.

## Sentry
A squat, stationary turret virus rooted to its panel — a rusted iron
pumpkin with a heavy riveted brow and a mouth fused shut. Its motion
sensor is a lamp-eye on a thin stalk that pops up from the crown and
burns red when the player enters its row. When it fires, the fused mouth
splits open along a glowing seam and the row laser pours out.

## Seedling
A small sprout virus with a cracked pumpkin-seed pod for a body, pale
green with dark mottling. A single curling vine sprout grows from the top
of its head, tipped with a budding thorn. It kneels to press seeds into
panels; planted seeds erupt into bramble vines — glossy black-green
tendrils studded with curved thorns. Cute at a glance, sinister on second
look.

## Mendbug
A plump, nervous medic virus — a round beetle body in soft mint green
with pale pink cross markings on its shell. Tiny translucent wings buzz
anxiously even when it's hovering still. Its antennae end in glowing
green repair-lights that pulse when it heals an ally. Always drifts
toward the back column, flinching away from the player's row.

## Jack
A lanky pumpkin-headed virus — an oversized jack-o'-lantern head with a
wide carved grin and triangular eyes burning orange, balanced on a
scarecrow-thin body of twisted black vines. Its long arms end in
three-fingered claws, usually cradling small round pumpkin bombs with
fizzing green stems. Winds its whole torso up like a pitcher before
lobbing.

## Pumpkiny
A small flower-fairy virus (see `assets/sprites/viruses/`). Orange petal
hair fanning out like a blooming pumpkin blossom, white fairy body, and a
black mask-like face with round yellow eyes. Translucent pale wings, thin
limbs, and a skirt of pointed orange petals. Drifts with glowing pollen
sparkles orbiting her. (Idle strip: `pumpkiny-idle-strip.png`, 16 frames,
56x87.)
